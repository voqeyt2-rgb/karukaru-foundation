Who We Are

KARUKARU FOUNDATION is a community-focused organization committed to improving lives and creating opportunities for young people and families.

We focus on sports, technology, education, youth empowerment and support for vulnerable families.

Our Vision

To build a stronger community where young people and families have opportunities to develop, grow and succeed.

Our Mission

To empower people through sports, technology, education and support for vulnerable families.

  • Discipline
